# Educational Framework

## 3.3 Relevance to Academics

### How the Game Helps Learners Develop New Knowledge or Skills
Learners develop historical and cultural literacy regarding Bhutanese *nyes*, iconography, and folklore. They also build critical reflection skills by assessing environmental conditions within the game and evaluating human impacts on sacred spaces.

### What Students Can Learn
*   1. Students will be able to identify key *nyes* (Jhomo Lhari, Drakay Pangtsho, Tak Tsang) and explain their historical and spiritual significance.
*   2. Students will understand the spiritual and ecological roles of deities/entities like *Aum Jomo* and *Tshomen*.
*   3. Students will articulate the concept of interdependence between human behavior and nature.

### The Target Audience
The primary target audience consists of Bhutanese youth, specifically students.

### How Gameplay Supports Teaching and Learning
*   **Jhomo Lhari:** Interaction with *Aum Jomo* teaches indigenous history, local geography, and deity worship.
*   **Drakay Pangtsho:** Encountering the *Tshomen* (mermaid) teaches traditional values regarding the sanctity, cleanliness, and protection of *tshos* (lakes).
*   **Tak Tsang:** Dialogues with monks pass down the historical and spiritual legacy of Guru Rinpoche and the heritage of the monastery.

### Implementation in Learning Environments
This game can be effectively introduced during Value Education, History, or Environmental Studies classes. Teachers can use it as a complementary interactive assignment or a baseline simulation activity before or after real-world field trips.

### Strategies to Maintain Learner Engagement and Motivation
Engagement is maintained through 2D RPG world exploration, expressive character dialogues, deity encounters, and visual progression as the player journeys through beautifully rendered sceneries of the *nyes*.

### Subject Integration
A core pillar of the curriculum integrated into this game is **Interdependence** and **Living in Coherence With Nature**. As players explore the varying environmental sceneries of each *Nye*, they are prompted by the game to reflect on the condition of the environment and what caused it to be in its current state. By learning how such actions affect sacred spaces, players realize how human behavior and nature are deeply interconnected, fostering an active mindset of living in coherence with the natural world.
